About SHREE BIJAY AUTOMOBILES

A partnership enterprise based in Pakur, Jharkhand, engaged in trading activities relating to motorcycles, scooters, mopeds and three-wheelers.

Who We Are

Business overview

SHREE BIJAY AUTOMOBILES is a partnership enterprise based in Pakur, Jharkhand, established in 2019 and engaged in trading activities related to motorcycles, scooters, mopeds, three-wheelers and related maintenance and repair.

The enterprise is registered under the Udyam framework with the classification “Small” for the 2025–26 period, and holds a regular GST registration. Its registered activity falls under NIC 4540 – Sale, maintenance and repair of motorcycles and related parts and accessories.

The principal place of business is located on the Pakur–Dumka Main Road near IndusInd Bank at Bhagatpara, Pakur. Two additional places of business are recorded in Pakur and Barharwa (Sahibganj district).

This page describes the enterprise only on the basis of its registration documents. No other business history, scale or affiliation is stated.
