About the Enterprise

A local trading enterprise in Pakur, Jharkhand

SHREE BIJAY AUTOMOBILES is a partnership enterprise based in Pakur, Jharkhand, established in 2019 and engaged in trading activities related to motorcycles, scooters, mopeds, three-wheelers and related maintenance and repair.

The enterprise operates from its principal place of business on the Pakur–Dumka Main Road at Bhagatpara, Pakur, with additional places of business registered in Pakur and Barharwa.

Business activity is classified under NIC 4540 – Sale, maintenance and repair of motorcycles and related parts and accessories.
